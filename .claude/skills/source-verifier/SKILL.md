# Source verifier
1. Identify the exact claim, date, place, and interpretation.
2. Locate the corresponding course source or primary/credible scholarly reference; record document identifier and location.
3. Distinguish source text, inference, and contested interpretation.
4. Compare contradictory sources; never resolve uncertainty by guessing.
5. Mark each claim VERIFIED, CONTESTED, or UNSUPPORTED.
6. Block publication of UNSUPPORTED claims and flag CONTESTED claims for human review.
7. Report citations and unresolved issues in Hebrew or French as appropriate.
